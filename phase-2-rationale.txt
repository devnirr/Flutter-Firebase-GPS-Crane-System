INSURANCE MODULE - BUDGET NOTE

WHY PHASE 2 COSTS WHAT IT DOES
================================================================================

Phase 1 puts the insurance companies on the platform.
Phase 2 takes your office out of the middle of it.

That is the whole difference, and it is where the money goes.

    Prepared for ..........  ______________________________
    Scope .................  Insurance module, phase 2
    Investment ............  US$5,500

THE SHORT ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 2 is not a set of extra screens. It is the work that lets the system
do, correctly and unsupervised, what a person in your office is doing by hand
after Phase 1 - and it is what makes a second, third and fourth insurance
company possible without hiring anyone.


WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE MONTHLY INVOICE
  After phase 1   Someone in your office generates the Excel, checks it line
                  by line, and sends it.
  After phase 2   The system closes the month and issues the invoice on its
                  own. Nobody touches it.

INSURANCE COMPANIES
  After phase 1   One, with its prices configured by us on request.
  After phase 2   As many as you sign, each with its own users, its own
                  zones, its own invoice.

THEIR STAFF
  After phase 1   Share a single login for the company.
  After phase 2   Each person has their own account, and no company can ever
                  see another's services.

PRICE ZONES
  After phase 1   Changed by the developer when a tariff changes.
  After phase 2   Managed by you from the panel, per company, the same day
                  they ask.

CORRECTIONS
  After phase 1   Edited by hand, with no record of what changed.
  After phase 2   Closed periods are locked; a correction is a numbered
                  credit note with an audit trail.

COMFORTABLE VOLUME
  After phase 1   Around 50 services a month, one company.
  After phase 2   500+ a month across several companies, with no extra
                  admin time.


WHERE THE THREE WEEKS GO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. AN INVOICE NOBODY HAS TO CHECK

   While you generate the Excel yourself, you are the safety net - you would
   notice a service billed twice. When the system closes the month alone,
   there is no net. So it needs the period locked once it closes, corrections
   issued as credit notes instead of quiet edits, and invoice numbers that
   never skip or repeat. These are accounting rules, not design work, and
   they are slow and unforgiving to build.

2. KEEPING THE COMPANIES APART

   Every insurance company must see its own services and nothing else - not a
   filter on a screen, but a rule enforced underneath, on every request. It is
   invisible when it works, and it is the first thing an insurer's own IT
   department asks about before they sign.

3. PRICES IN YOUR HANDS, NOT OURS

   Each insurer negotiates its own tariff by kilometre zone, and they revise
   it. Phase 2 puts that table in your panel so a change takes you ten
   minutes, instead of an email to us and a wait.

4. ROOM TO SIGN THE NEXT THREE

   Phase 1 proves the model with one client. Phase 2 is what lets you answer
   "yes" when the next one asks whether you can absorb 200 services a month -
   without that answer costing you a salary.


THE INVESTMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                2 weeks of support after launch included

If you prefer to spread it, phase 2 can be split in two, in this order:

    Automatic monthly close and invoicing ........  US$3,000 - 2 weeks
    Self-service portal, users and zone tariffs ..  US$2,800 - 2 weeks

Split, it comes to US$300 more and takes a week longer - setting the work
down and picking it up again is not free. It is still the right choice if the

IF YOU WOULD RATHER NOT, THAT IS A REAL OPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 1 works on its own. You can stay on it indefinitely. The cost simply
moves: instead of a one-time fee, it becomes a few days of somebody's time
every month, forever, plus the invoices that get disputed because they were
typed by a person at the end of a long week. Practically, it caps you at one
or two insurance clients.

Phase 2 is the decision to stop paying that monthly cost, and to make room
for the clients after the first one.
